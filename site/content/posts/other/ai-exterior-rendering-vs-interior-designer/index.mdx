## Paying for "Design"

When I started planning the exterior, I got quotes from interior designers.
Thousands of dollars.
For what? Some concepts? A few renders?
I'm building in the Philippines. Every peso I save on a "concept" is a peso I can spend on actual steel or cement.

So I skipped the designer.
I used AI.
Cost me $20.

![AI Exterior Rendering Example](https://github.com/danielsobrado/buildinginthephilippines/blob/main/images/SoraRenders1.jpg?raw=true)

## The Toolkit (Cheap)

### Sora + ChatGPT
I pay for ChatGPT Plus ($20/month). It has Sora.
I upload my ugly sketch or a basic boxy 3D model.
I tell it: "Make this look like a tropical modern house with wood accents."
Boom. 
It gives me 4 variations in 30 seconds.

It's actually absurdly good.
- "Show it at sunset" -> Done.
- "Change wood to stone" -> Done.
- "Add a Ferrari in the driveway" -> Done (I can't afford the Ferrari, but it looks nice).

![Sora Rendering Variations](https://github.com/danielsobrado/buildinginthephilippines/blob/main/images/SoraRenders2.jpg?raw=true)

### Nano Banana (Google)
For tweaking. It's free.
I use this when Sora goes too crazy and adds extra floors I didn't ask for.
You can adjust the "temperature" so it sticks closer to your original drawing.

![Nano Banana Interface](https://github.com/danielsobrado/buildinginthephilippines/blob/main/images/NanoBanana1.jpg?raw=true)

## How to actually get good results

Don't say "make a nice house".
AI is dumb if you are vague.

Be specific:
- "Photorealistic architectural rendering, professional lighting, DAY scene"
- "Industrial materials: dark metal cladding, exposed concrete"
- "Bronze aluminum windows, grey tint glass"

If you use technical terms ("cladding", "soffit", "stucco"), it thinks you are an architect and gives you better results.

## The Good vs The Bad

**The Good:**
- It is essentially free.
- fast.
- You can try 50 ideas in an hour.

**The Bad:**
- It hallucinates. Sometimes it puts a window in a column.
- It is NOT a blueprint. Do not give an AI render to your foreman and say "build this". He will laugh at you. Or worse, he will try to build it and the house will collapse.

![AI Rendering Results](https://github.com/danielsobrado/buildinginthephilippines/blob/main/images/SoraRenders3.jpg?raw=true)

## Verdict

I am not saying "fire all architects".
You need a pro for the structure, the plumbing, the actual plans.
But for *visualizing*? For deciding "do I want grey or beige paint?"
Don't pay someone $2000 for that.
Use the robot.
