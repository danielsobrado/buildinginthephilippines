## Step 1: The Team
You need 3 people.
**1. The Architect:** The dreamer. Makes it look good.
**2. The Engineer:** The realist. Makes sure it stands up.
**3. The Contractor:** The doer. Actually builds it.

Sometimes 1 and 2 are the same firm.
Sometimes 1, 2, and 3 are the same guy (Design-Build).
**My advice:** Keep the Designer and the Builder separate.
You want the Designer to check the Builder's work.
If they are the same person, who checks who?

## Step 2: The Paper Chase
Before you dig a hole, you need:
- Barangay Clearance
- Locational Clearance
- Fire Clearance
- Building Permit
- Electrical Permit
- Sanitary Permit
- HOA Permit (if in a subdivision)

Expect this to take 1-3 months.
Don't bribe. It just makes them ask for more next time.

## Step 3: The Design Phase
Don't just say "I want a modern house".
Show pictures.
Discuss the **wind** and the **sun**.
"Sir, the sun sets here. If we put a big glass window here, my bedroom will be an oven."
Think about the rain. Where does the water go?

## Step 4: The Bidding
Take your plans.
Give them to 3 different contractors.
You will get 3 wildly different prices.
**Contractor A:** 3M (Too cheap. suspicious).
**Contractor B:** 5M (Reasonable).
**Contractor C:** 8M (Dreaming).

Ask Contractor A why he is so cheap. (Did he forget the roof?).
Ask Contractor C why he is so expensive. (Is he using gold toilets?).

## Summary
It's a marathon.
Take your time in the planning phase.
Changing a line on paper costs 1 peso of ink.
Changing a concrete wall costs 50,000 pesos.
Plan well.
