## The "Tree" vs The "Octopus"
Most Filipino plumbers build a "Tree".
One big pipe enters the house.
It splits (Tee) to the kitchen.
Splits again to the CR.
Splits again to the shower.
**The problem:** When someone flushes the toilet, your shower gets scalding hot because the pressure dropped.

## The "Octopus" (Manifold)
I saw this in Germany and it changed my life.
You have a central hub (Manifold).
One dedicated pipe goes to the Kitchen.
One dedicated pipe goes to the Shower.
One dedicated pipe goes to the Toilet.
**The result:** You can flush the toilet 10 times and the person in the shower won't scream.

## Why don't we use it here?
Cost. mostly.
You need way more pipe.
But pipe is cheap.
Labor is the same.
The real reason? **Plumbers are lazy.**
It's easier to cut a pipe and stick a Tee in it than to run a new line back to the source.

## PEX vs PVC
If you go Manifold, you should probably use PEX (flexible plastic pipe).
Why? Because you can bend it.
No elbows = No leaks under the tiles.
If you use PVC (Blue pipe), you need 50 elbows to run a manifold system. That's 50 places it can leak.

## My Advice
If you are building a small house? The traditional "Tree" is fine.
If you have 3 bathrooms and a washing machine?
Get a manifold.
Or enjoy your cold showers.
