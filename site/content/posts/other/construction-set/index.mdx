## Paying 50k vs 500k for plans
You see an architect on FB: "Complete plans 20k!"
Then you see a firm charging 500k.
What gives?

## The "20k Special"
Usually just 5-7 sheets.
- Floor plan (basic)
- Elevations (front/back/side)
- A generic plumbing layout that won't work.
This is enough to get a permit (sometimes).
It is NOT enough to build a house properly.
Your contractor will guess everything else.
And when contractors guess, they guess "expensive" or "wrong".

## The Real Deal (What you actually need)
If you want a house that doesn't collapse, you need these:

**1. The "Boring" Structural Details**
- Beam details (How much stirrup spacing? 10mm every 15cm?)
- Column footing details (Don't let them just dig a hole and pour cement).
- Roof framing (Welding specs. Most welders just spot weld... dangerous).

**2. The Reflected Ceiling Plan**
Fancy name for "Where do the lights go?"
Without this, the electrician will just put one ugly bulb in the center of every room.

**3. The Spec Sheet**
This is the bible.
- "6063 Aluminum" (Not random scrap metal).
- "Class A mixture for columns" (Not the weak stuff).
- "THHN wire" (Not cheap automotive wire).

## The Architect Hunt
Don't just look at their renders.
Ask to see a previous "Construction Set" they made.
Does it have 30 pages? or 5?
If it's 5 pages... run.
or prepare to be the site architect yourself.
