## Attic Insulation: The Real Deal

Look, everyone overcomplicates this.
You have a hot attic. You want a cool bedroom. That's it.
Spent weeks researching R-values and materials because contractors here will just tell you to use "foam" (useless).

Basically you have two options. well, two good ones.

### option 1: the fancy way (Roof Insulation)
This is where you stick insulation right under the roof sheets.
It creates a "warm roof" - terrible name, it actually keeps the attic cool.

**Why bother:**
- If you have water tanks or pipes up there, they won't boil
- Your attic becomes actual usable storage
- condensation isn't as big of a deal

**The catch:**
- Expensive. like, 2x the cost.
- you need way more material
- if you don't vent it properly you get mold... saw this happen to a neighbor in Cavite. gross.

### option 2: the cheap way (Floor Insulation)
Just roll some insulation on the attic floor / ceiling of your room.

**Why I like this:**
- Way cheaper. less surface area.
- Works great if you just want a cold bedroom
- easy to DIY if you're brave enough to go into a hot attic

**The downside:**
- Your attic stays oven-hot (like 50C+)
- Don't store candles up there. or plastic.
- You can't really use it as a room

## finding the right stuff

Forget the brand names for a second. here's what you'll actually find in hardware stores:

**Rockwool (Mineral Wool)**
- best bang for buck usually
- won't catch fire (melts at >1000C or something crazy)
- easy to cut
- ITCHY AS HELL. wear gloves. seriously.

**XPS (That pink/blue foam board)**
- Stiff, you can walk on it
- handles water better
- expensive though
- termites might eat it? heard mixed stories on this

## vapor barriers?
Everyone asks about this.
"Do I need a plastic sheet?"
In the Philippines... usually no.
Unless you have 24/7 AC making your room freezing while it's 35C outside, condensation isn't the main enemy here.
It's the heat. focus on the heat.

## my advice
If you're just storing christmas decors... go with floor insulation.
Use Rockwool.
Save the money for better windows or something.

But if you want to turn that attic into a man cave? yeah you need the roof insulation.
Just prepare your wallet.

btw wear a mask if you DIY this. attic dust is no joke.
