## The Big 3

**1. The Gable (Triangle)**
Classic house shape.
**Pros:** Easy to build. Good ventilation (Gable vents).
**Cons:** The flat wall of the triangle catches the wind. Typhoon hits it -> Wall shakes.

**2. The Hip (Pyramid)**
Slopes on all 4 sides.
**Pros:** The king of Typhoon resistance. Wind flows over it like water.
**Cons:** Harder to build. More "cutting" of sheets (waste). Harder to ventilate (Need ridge vents).

**3. The Flat (Deck)**
**Pros:** You can party on it.
**Cons:** It leaks. (See my other article).
It also gets HOT. Concrete absorbs heat all day and releases it into your bedroom at night.

## My Advice
If you are in a typhoon path (Samar, Leyte, Bicol)...
Build a **Hip Roof**.
It's more expensive (more trusses, more cuts).
But it stays attached to your house.

If you are in a "safe" area...
**Gable** is fine.
Just make sure the eaves aren't too wide.
