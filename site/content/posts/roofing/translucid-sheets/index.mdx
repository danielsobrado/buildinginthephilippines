## "I want natural light!"
So you put a clear roof over your laundry area.
3 months later...
It's yellow. It's brittle. And it has a hole from a falling mango.
What happened?

## The Material Hierarchy

**1. Solid Polycarbonate (The King)**
It looks like glass.
It's bulletproof (basically).
It has UV coating.
**Cost:** Expensive.
**Lifespan:** 10+ years.
**Verdict:** Buy this one.

**2. Twin-Wall Polycarbonate (The Trap)**
It looks like a plastic sandwich with holes.
**The Problem:** Water gets inside the holes. Algae grows.
It turns green and gross inside the sheet. You can't clean it.
Avoid this unless you seal the edges perfectly (nobody does).

**3. Fiberglass (The Classic)**
It's okay.
It's tough.
But the cheap ones turn yellow fast.
And the fibers start to stick out (itchy).

## Heat Warning
Clear roof = Greenhouse.
If you enclose a room with a clear roof and no vents...
You are baking your laundry.
You need airflow.
And maybe get "Bronze" or "White" tint instead of clear. It blocks some heat.

## Summary
Solid Polycarbonate.
Yes, it costs double.
But you won't have to replace it when you accidentally step on it.
