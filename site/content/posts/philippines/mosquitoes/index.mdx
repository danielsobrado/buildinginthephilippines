## The Public Enemy #1
In the Philippines, mosquitoes aren't annoying. They are dangerous.
Dengue. Malaria.
You need to design your house to fight them.

## 1. Screens are non-negotiable.
"I want an open air villa!"
No you don't.
You will be eaten alive at 5pm.
Screen every window.
Use **Stainless Steel Mesh** (Security + Bug proof).
Nylon mesh rots in the sun after 2 years.

## 2. The Color of your House
Mosquitoes love dark corners.
If you paint your walls dark grey (very trendy right now)...
You are creating a hangout spot.
Paint the exterior light colors. They hate it.

## 3. The Seal
Door sweeps.
Those little bristles at the bottom of the door?
Install them.
Mosquitoes will crawl under the door.

## 4. The Gutter
This is where they are born.
Leaf falls in gutter -> Water gets trapped -> Mosquito laid eggs.
Use a **Gutter Guard** (mesh over the gutter).
Or better yet... don't use gutters.
Use a concrete trench drain on the ground.
Gravity never clogs.

## Summary
You can't kill them all.
But you can keep them out.
Screens on windows.
Sweeps on doors.
No standing water.
Simple.
