## What is ICF?
Imagine building a house out of giant styrofoam Lego blocks.
Then you put steel bars inside.
Then you fill the hollow space with concrete.
That's ICF (Insulated Concrete Forms).

## Why build a styrofoam house?
It sounds crazy, but the styrofoam stays on the wall forever.
**1. It's Cool (Literally).**
You have insulation on the outside AND inside.
It's like living inside an ice chest.
Your AC bill drops by 50%.

**2. It's Tough.**
Inside that foam is solid reinforced concrete.
Typhoon winds? They bounce off.
Earthquakes? It's solid concrete.

## The Problem (Cost)
It's expensive.
About 40% more than hollow blocks.
And finding a contractor in the Philippines who knows how to do it? Good luck.
If they mess up the pour, the foam blows out and you have concrete all over your garden.

## Types of ICF
**1. EPS (Styrofoam)** - The standard. Cheap, effective.
**2. Comparisons** - Honestly, just stick to standard EPS blocks available locally. Importing specialized polyurethane forms will bankrupt you.

## Summary
If you hate heat and have money? Go ICF.
If you are on a budget? Stick to hollow blocks (but insulate your roof!).
