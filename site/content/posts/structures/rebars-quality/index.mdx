## Are you buying fake steel?
In Mindanao, they just found a huge batch of "substandard rebars".
Basically, fake steel.
If you use this in your columns... good luck in the next earthquake.

## How to check (The Cheat Sheet)
You don't need a lab. You need a scale.

**1. Weigh it.**
Bring a portable hanging scale to the hardware store.
Weigh a 1-meter sample.
- **10mm bar:** should be **0.616 kg**
- **12mm bar:** should be **0.888 kg**
- **16mm bar:** should be **1.58 kg**

If it's lighter? It's "undersized".
They sell it as 10mm, but it's actually 9mm.
That 10% difference means your house is 20% weaker.

**2. The Magnet Test**
Rebar is iron. Magnets stick to it.
If the magnet barely sticks? It causes it has too many impurities. Garbage.

**3. The Bend Test**
Grab a 10mm bar. Try to bend it with your hands (use a pipe for leverage if you are small).
It should be tough.
If it bends like a wire hanger? It's too soft.
If it snaps instantly? It's too brittle.

## Summary
Don't trust the sticker.
Don't trust the hardware store guy saying "Sir, standard yan!"
Weigh it.
If the weight is wrong, walk away.
